# Design system and product ecosystem
Approved direction: one institution, multiple coherent product experiences, inspired by Google's product architecture, not a copy of Google's visual identity.

## Brand
Aunty Alice Schools is the institutional brand; Aunty Alice Primary and Secondary are school identities; Alice Academy is the national learning product; Alice Portal is the private operational product. Alice Shop, Library and CBT are named capabilities within their parent products, not separate deployments. Shared wordmark family, orange #F26B0F, ink #141110, paper #FFF7F0, white and cool accent #175B77. Use accessible contrast, focus, semantic navigation and 44px preferred touch targets. Final logo requires school approval.

## Experiences
- Main: premium editorial, cinematic approved campus video, admissions, authentic evidence, school life.
- Primary: playful, warm, age-appropriate illustrations and rounded components.
- Secondary: mature, aspirational editorial design, academics, leadership, science and sports.
- Academy: fast, utility-first, search, exam tracks, subjects, notes, practice and progress.
- Portal (M4): task-first private dashboard for families and staff.

## Shared product switcher
An accessible nine-dot launcher in the shared header, with labelled product tiles and keyboard navigation, focus restoration, Escape dismissal and clear current-product indication. Only available destinations appear; never populate fake links to imply operational services. Ordinary navigation remains available. On mobile use a compact accessible popover or sheet. Root homepage also provides intent-based entry points for admissions, exam prep, resources and (later) family services.

## Mobile and media
Design at 360px; test 320/360/390/768/desktop. Poster-first hero, approved compressed muted video enhancement only when appropriate, reduced-motion and data-saving fallback, playback pause control, no autoplay audio or blocking video download. No external video iframe until activated. Aim for 200 KiB initial compressed route transfer excluding deferred video, measure and report. No fabricated school photography or facts.

## Navigation
Root: home, about, admissions, primary, secondary, school life, news, shop, contact, privacy, safeguarding. School sections: academics, staff, calendar, activities, admissions. Academy: exam, subject, topic, lesson, practice, library, search and later CBT/downloads. Portal deferred.
