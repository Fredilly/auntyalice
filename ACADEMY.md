# Alice Academy
Free national learning experience, visibly powered by Aunty Alice Schools. No compulsory sign-in for browsing. Exam -> subject -> topic -> lesson -> practice -> explanation -> next step. Initial tracks JAMB/UTME, WAEC/WASSCE, NECO, BECE, Common Entrance. Each receives a distinct landing page, applicable syllabus edition, subjects and progress context; shared canonical lesson library prevents duplication.

## Content and publication
Teacher records focused 5–10 minute video, submits school-owned YouTube URL, text summary, objective, worked examples and original questions. Academic reviewer checks accuracy, rights, child safeguarding, audience designation and accessibility before publishing. Click-to-load video; text alternative mandatory. No private footage on unlisted YouTube. Original practice questions with explanations; reproduce past papers only with documented permission/lawful basis. Official syllabuses mapped separately; no implied affiliation. Mathematics pilot first, other tracks visible with honest coverage states.

## Roadmap
Search, notes, reviewed question bank, timed mocks, offline public bundles, optional learner progress after safeguarding review. No public individual leaderboards, unsupervised messaging or accredited certificate claims. Later aggregate opt-in school challenges and moderated Q&A.
