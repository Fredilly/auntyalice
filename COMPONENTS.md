# Component standards — Design System V2
Initial Academy card/navigation components are implemented; shared brand and typography refinements remain to be implemented. Start at 360px with compact spacing, legible text, consistent radii, restrained elevation, semantic tokens and visible focus. Use shared primitives rather than page-specific copies. Icons should convey meaning, have accessible labels where needed and never rely on color alone.

Exam cards: icon, exam name, short level/description, clear destination; muted accent per track (blue JAMB, orange WAEC, green NECO, violet BECE, gold Common Entrance), contrast tested. Two columns only when content fits at phone width; otherwise one. Subject cards: meaningful icon, subject name, honest availability; lesson cards: title, topic, objective, reviewed/preview status, real duration/progress only when known. School cards: distinct editorial identity and approved media; activity cards: verified details, not fictional events. Entire navigation card is one semantic link; no nested buttons. Avoid repetitive empty placeholders.

Search: labelled input, useful matching, clear action and empty state. Navigation: shared product switcher plus local destinations; active indication, accessible keyboard and Escape behavior, bottom bar safe-area spacing; desktop rail after mobile validation. Buttons: primary/secondary/quiet styles, loading and disabled states, minimum preferred 44px target. Forms: explicit labels, field-level errors, no unnecessary personal details. Feedback: immediate correct/incorrect plus explanation, non-color cues, accessible status; progress reflects actual answers. Include loading, empty, error, retry and not-found states with next steps.

Acceptance: actual screenshots at 320/360/390 then 768/1440, no overflow, keyboard/focus and touch tests, route/interaction checks, contrast and initial transfer measurements. Report visual cues and exact URLs. No component is considered complete on a mockup alone.

## Global brand and component tokens
Brand mark: orange square or softly rounded box #F26B0F with centered white capital A #FFFFFF, no dot, gradient or lowercase variant. Reuse a single mark across header, footer, launcher, favicon and product surfaces; pair with a compact Aunty Alice wordmark where appropriate. Provide accessible link names; decorative marks hidden from assistive technology. Source Serif 4 for institutional headings; Public Sans for interface/body/navigation; local optimized fonts with Georgia/Arial fallbacks. Ivory #FAF8F3, ink #141110, white #FFFFFF, academic blue #175B77, deep green #203B3B. Use dark text on orange actions where required for WCAG contrast. Prefer restrained corners, quiet secondary buttons, moderate heading sizes and consistent spacing instead of generic AI startup aesthetics. Preserve all existing functional card/navigation and mobile-first acceptance requirements. Staff interfaces remain calm and operational.
