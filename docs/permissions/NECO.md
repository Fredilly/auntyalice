# Permission request — NECO past questions

**To:** NECO Examinations / Digital Services
**From:** Alice Academy (Aunty Alice Schools), Mararaba, Nasarawa State
**Subject:** Permission to reproduce NECO past examination questions on a free school platform

We are a secondary school in Mararaba, Nasarawa State, Nigeria, building a free
learning platform — Alice Academy — for our own students and for candidates preparing
for national examinations. Students work through lessons, practice questions and timed
mock examinations on the platform.

We are asking for permission to reproduce past examination questions, and we want to be
straightforward about what we intend:

- We will host the questions ourselves so students can answer them inside the platform,
  rather than being sent to third-party websites.
- Every question will be attributed to NECO, the year, the paper and the question number.
- We will not sell the questions or place them behind payment. The Academy is free to use.
- We will link back to neco.gov.ng as the authoritative source, and state that the papers remain
  the copyright of NECO.
- We will remove any material immediately on request.

If a formal licence, a permission letter, or a standard set of conditions exists, we will
follow it. If the answer is no, we will not host the papers and will continue to link to
your official resources instead — we would simply rather ask than assume.

Questions:
1. Do you permit reproduction of past questions on a free educational platform?
2. If yes, for which years and subjects, and under what conditions or fee?
3. Is there a preferred attribution wording you require?
4. Do you have a digital or API source for recent papers we should use instead of scanning?
