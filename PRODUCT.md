# Product
Aunty Alice Schools, Mararaba, Nasarawa, Nigeria. Build a premium school institution and a free national education service in one ecosystem. Public school wins enrolments; academy helps learners; portal improves school operations. Different journeys, shared brand.

## Scope
M1: public homepage, primary/secondary, admissions, school life, academy discovery for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, BECE and Common Entrance. All tracks represented honestly; reviewed content grows incrementally. M2: teacher CMS, approved videos, mathematics pilot and catalogue. M3: exam-specific syllabus coverage, practice explanations, timed CBT, offline materials. M4: confirmed family/staff workflows, school ledger integration and payments. M5: mastery, moderated Q&A, aggregate opt-in competitions, partnerships.

Public learners browse without an account. Teachers submit lessons for review. Parents discover school and enquire through verified WhatsApp/contact alternatives. Clubs have approved media, coordinators, events and supervised joining where confirmed. Shop starts as catalogue; no duplicate inventory. No unverified results, contacts, claims or testimonials. No public individual rankings or unsupervised child messaging.
