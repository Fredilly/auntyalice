# Product specification
Aunty Alice Schools, Mararaba, Nasarawa, Nigeria. One institution, multiple experiences: institutional website wins enrolments; primary is warm and playful; secondary is academic and aspirational; Alice Academy is a fast national learning utility; Alice Portal is a private school operating system (M4). Shared brand, not identical interfaces. Shop, Library and CBT are capabilities, not independent domains.

Audiences: prospective parents discover authentic school evidence and apply; current families find news/calendar/resources and later fees/results/orders; public learners study and practise without login; teachers submit lessons and club updates; leaders approve publications and manage permissions. WhatsApp admissions plus accessible telephone/email alternatives, all verified. No personal information collected on placeholder forms.

M1: public school pages, primary/secondary, admissions, school life, academy discovery across JAMB, WAEC, NECO, BECE and Common Entrance. M2: staff CMS, reviewed mathematics pilot, YouTube, news and lightweight book/uniform catalogue. M3: verified syllabus breadth, original question banks, explanations, timed CBT, offline study, optional accounts after safeguarding review. M4: confirmed workflows for guardians, attendance, timetable, assignments, results, fees, receipts and School Ledger integration. Avoid duplicate inventory; catalogue/WhatsApp first, click-and-collect and payments later. M5: mastery, revision, moderated Q&A, opt-in aggregate inter-school challenges, completion recognition (not accreditation), partnerships and premium mocks/tutoring only after validation. AI tutoring requires evaluation and safeguards.

School life: each confirmed club has coordinator, approved media, schedule, event feed and supervised joining. Candidates: drama, sports/houses, agriculture/farm diary if verified, science/STEM, debate, music, art, chess, entrepreneurship and cultural activities. Produce sales only with approval. Do not imply any club, farm, result or facility exists before verification.

Measure admissions enquiries and applications, mobile task completion, reviewed lessons/week, syllabus coverage, practice completion and learning gains, repeat learning and teacher publishing success. Establish baselines before targets. Core learning remains free. No public individual leaderboards, unsupervised child messaging or behavioral ad tracking.
